## The social impact ecosystem

We started as a group of young people who cared about social service. Out of that
came the purpose of building a digital bridge between those who want to help and
those who need it most.

---

## We have the courage to serve and transform lives

The name comes from **coraje** — courage. The heart replacing the *o* carries
**love**. Our mark is built to transmit courage, innovation and love.

Courage here is not bravado. It is showing up when it is inconvenient, putting
your name on a delivery, and publishing the account afterwards. That last part is
what connects the idea to the product: named responsables, direct transfer and
moderated evidence are courage turned into a mechanism. Saying you are
transparent is easy; publishing the receipt takes courage.

Corag exists because of a simple reality: plenty of people want to give or get
involved, but cannot find a way to do it that feels trustworthy, close and
meaningful. Generosity is not the scarce resource — connection is.

---

## Our values

- **Collaboration** — We bring governments, organizations, companies and
  individuals into one network instead of multiplying isolated systems.
- **Empathy** — Someone receiving aid is a neighbour, not the backdrop for
  somebody else's virtue.
- **Trust** — Every contribution has a named person behind it and a destination
  you can check.
- **Love** — It is in the heart at the centre of the mark, and in the reason any
  of this exists.
- **Social innovation** — A public API, an OpenAPI specification and an MCP
  server, so others can build on the same network.
- **Transparency** — We publish how much came in and how much was used with
  evidence. Two separate numbers, both public.

---

## In short

- Corag is the ecosystem; Ayuda Directa is the flagship product.
- Coordination, contributions and evidence all happen in the application.
- Money moves by direct transfer to verified responsables.
- Evidence is moderated before it is published.
- A public API, OpenAPI 3.1 and an MCP server are open to any integrator.

---

## The two surfaces

- **cabuya.org** — this site. It explains what Corag is, how it works, and how to
  integrate with it.
- **ayuda.cabuya.org** — **Ayuda Directa**, the flagship product. All the
  coordination happens there: needs, offers, contributions, leaders and evidence.

If something holds information about a real person's need, it lives in the
application.
