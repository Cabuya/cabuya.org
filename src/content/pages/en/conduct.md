## Why this exists

Corag coordinates aid between people who are often having a bad time. That makes
how we treat each other part of the service, not a side note.

This code applies to **every Corag space** — conversations inside operational
fronts, public channels, in-person events, code repositories, and any interaction
where someone is acting on Corag's behalf.

---

## What we expect

- **Treat someone receiving aid as a neighbour, not as a case.** Nobody is the
  backdrop for another person's goodwill.
- **Be clear and direct.** In an emergency, ambiguity costs time somebody does
  not have.
- **Assume good intent, and check the facts.** Almost everyone here is donating
  their time.
- **Credit other people's work.** If someone coordinated, delivered or documented
  something, say so by name.
- **When you get it wrong, correct it in public.** Publishing the correction is
  worth more than being right.

---

## What is not acceptable

- **Publishing other people's personal data** — phone numbers, addresses, exact
  locations, identifiable photos — without their explicit authorization. This is
  the most important rule here, and "it was urgent" is not an exception.
- **Using an emergency for personal gain**: self-promotion, lead generation,
  political or religious proselytizing, or soliciting contributions to an account
  Corag has not verified.
- **Harassment in any form**: comments about someone's body, gender, orientation,
  religion, origin, disability or financial situation; unwanted persistence;
  unsolicited sexual attention.
- **Impersonating a person or organization**, or presenting yourself as a Corag
  responsable when you are not one.
- **Publishing false or unverified information** about a need, a delivery or a
  contribution.
- **Threats or incitement to violence**, on or off the platform.

---

## Personal data: the operating rule

Operational fronts coordinate real aid, and that sometimes requires sharing a
contact. The rule is simple:

1. **Coordination stays inside the platform.** Do not post phone numbers or email
   addresses in conversations.
2. **A contact is published only with that person's explicit authorization**, and
   only when the coordination genuinely needs it.
3. **Anyone contributing money may appear as "Persona solidaria"** instead of by
   name. Respect that choice without asking why.
4. **Never publish the exact location of someone in a vulnerable situation**
   beyond what the platform already shows in order to coordinate the delivery.

---

## Responsables and leaders

Coordinating resources is a responsibility, not a privilege:

- Account for what you received, with evidence, within the window.
- Do not use the position to direct resources toward your own interests.
- Hand the coordination over cleanly if you step away from the role.

Repeated failures — especially not providing evidence — can end in suspension
from the role.

---

## How to report

If you witness or experience something that breaks this code, report it. You can
do so **anonymously** using the form on this page.

What happens next:

1. We receive the report and treat it as confidential.
2. We review what happened, hearing the parties involved where appropriate.
3. We respond proportionately: a conversation, a warning, suspension from a role,
   or removal from Corag spaces.
4. If you left a way to contact you, we tell you how it was resolved.

**You do not have to be sure it "qualifies".** If something felt wrong, that is
reason enough to write it down.

> ⚠️ If anyone is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services
> first. This channel is not an emergency service.

---

## Scope

This code applies to anyone taking part in Corag spaces, regardless of role,
seniority, or connection to a partner organization. It applies equally to the
person coordinating, the person contributing, and the person receiving.
