The social impact ecosystem.

**Corag** connects people who want to help with those who need it most, so aid

## The intent is there. The connection is what fails.

The problem.

Plenty of people want to give or get involved, but cannot find a way to do it
that feels trustworthy, close and meaningful. Generosity is not the scarce
resource — connection is. Aid ends up scattered across places that never talk
to each other.

- WhatsApp threads
- One-off forms
- Spreadsheets
- Phone calls
- Posts that get buried
- Lists nobody updates

---

## A bridge between the person who needs and the person who can

How it works.

A need is published, located and categorized. Someone who can help finds it. It
gets coordinated, delivered, and what happened is on the record.

1. **It is published** — A person or community records what they need, where,
   and how urgently. It stays visible to the network, not buried in a chat.
2. **A responsable takes it on** — Someone with a name, a validated identity and
   a verified connection to the area owns the delivery.
3. **Someone contributes** — The contribution goes by direct transfer to the
   responsable's verified account, with no intermediaries.
4. **It is delivered** — The responsable buys, transports and delivers, and
   records what was delivered, to how many people, and when.
5. **The evidence stays** — Photos and receipts go through review, and only then
   get published. Until then the contribution counts as received, not as used.

## Saying you are transparent is easy. Publishing the receipt takes courage.

Transparency.

That is why every contribution has a named person behind it and every peso has
a destination you can check. We publish two separate numbers, and both are
public.

- **Received** — How much came in. It is the easy number to show, and on its own
  it says nothing.
- **Used with evidence** — How much was spent with verified proof: receipts,
  photos, results. This is the number that matters.
- **Direct transfer** — Money goes to verified leaders' accounts, not to a
  central treasury. Every contribution is attributable.

The real numbers live in the application, always current. Every contribution is
attributable to a responsable and to its evidence.

## Many interfaces, one network of data

Interoperability.

During an emergency several applications appear at once, each with its own
database of needs. The result is more fragmentation, not less. That is why we
publish an open API: so a new application becomes a client of the same network
rather than another silo.

[Developer documentation](/en/developers)

`POST /api/public/v1/help`

```json
{
  "source": "mi-app",
  "externalId": "ejemplo-001",
  "type": "offer",
  "title": "Ejemplo: mercados disponibles",
  "category": "alimentos",
  "emergencySlug": "eje-cafetero",
  "contactName": "Nombre de ejemplo",
  "contactWhatsapp": "+57 300 000 0000",
  "publishContact": true
}
```

Real API categories: `alimentos`, `salud`, `refugio`, `transporte`, `acopio`,
`rescate`, `otro`.
