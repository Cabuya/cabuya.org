You want to take part in something concrete this month. You search for volunteering events and find WhatsApp groups, buried posts, expired dates nobody updated, and forms that never get answered.

There is no shortage of events. Every month there are dozens of active sessions across the country's main cities. What is missing is one place holding them all.

## The three steps

### 1. Find a centralised source

Searching general social media does not work: the algorithm does not prioritise this content. Searching the web does not either: the results are out of date.

What works are sources that validate the organizations and keep calendars with the date, place and concrete need for each session.

### 2. Filter by your real availability

How many hours do you have this month? Which weekends are free? Can you travel outside your city or only within it? Do you have a skill that adds particular value?

Answering that **before** signing up prevents committing to something you will not follow through on — and that no-show costs someone else a place.

### 3. Sign up in advance

Serious sessions have limited places for logistical reasons.

If you find out the day before, there is probably no space. A week ahead gives you a real chance. With a calendar you review monthly, you get to choose.

## The habit that changes everything

Anyone who manages to take part month after month for years shares one thing: **they treat volunteering as a scheduled commitment, not as a flexible option.**

If you wait to feel inspired before signing up, you will rarely sign up. If you check the calendar in the first days of each month and book your place, consistency becomes automatic.

The volunteers with the most impact are not the most emotionally motivated. They are the most systematic.

## Two things that help

**Bring someone.** When the activity is shared — with a friend, a partner, a colleague — the odds of following through rise considerably. And you add one more person to the ecosystem.

**If you are a company, plan ahead.** Monthly corporate volunteering works when it is coordinated with organizations that know how to receive groups and integrated into normal operations. Improvised, it feels imposed and lasts two months.
