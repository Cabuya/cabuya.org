Anyone can now connect with any cause from a phone. That abundance creates a new problem: which ones are reliable, which actually connect you to real organizations, and which are digital appearance with nothing verifiable behind them.

## The five types

### 1. Volunteering

They connect people with opportunities to give time. Their value is in the curation: if they list everything that exists without filtering, they hand you back the search-engine problem.

### 2. Donations

They facilitate transfers to verified organizations. The key question is **who verifies and against what criteria** — not everyone using the word "verified" did the same work.

### 3. Campaigns

They let people and organizations raise money for a specific cause. They are the most useful in a concrete emergency and also the easiest to use for fraud, because anyone can open one.

### 4. Corporate social impact

They coordinate companies' social responsibility programmes: corporate volunteering, institutional donations, partnerships.

### 5. Integrated ecosystems

They combine several of the above: centralising opportunities, measuring impact, building community, and connecting different actors in one network.

It is the model the sector is moving toward, and also where it is easiest to promise more than gets delivered.

## What to ask before signing up

- **Who verifies the listed organizations, and how?** If there is no public answer, the word "verified" means nothing.
- **What does it measure, and does it publish what it measures?** A platform that only counts user registrations is not measuring impact.
- **What happens to my data?** Particularly if you are going to publish a need of your own.
- **Is it active?** Check the date of the last real activity, not the last marketing post.

## The most expensive mistake

Signing up to five platforms and using none of them properly.

These tools work like any network: value grows with consistent use and integration into the active community. Five abandoned profiles produce nothing. One active profile produces something.

Choose one that fits how you live and give it real time.

## What no platform replaces

Presence. A good platform cuts the gap between wanting to help and finding where from weeks to minutes. After that you have to show up, and come back.

If a tool promises you impact without ever appearing anywhere, it is selling the feeling, not the result.
