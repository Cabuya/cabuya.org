You live in Pereira and you want to help. You look for information and find stale pages, numbers that no longer work, and foundations that closed years ago.

Pereira has an active community of people helping; the problem was never a shortage of causes. It was the absence of a clear channel between those who want to help and those who need it.

## Where the projects are concentrated

Areas such as **Cuba**, **Villa Santana**, **San Joaquín**, **El Plumón** and the rural parts of **Combia** hold much of the active work.

Each has small and mid-sized foundations sustaining the daily lives of hundreds of families on very limited resources.

## The five fields that need support year-round

### Children outside school hours

Many working families need their children to have help with homework, extra tutoring, or simply a safe place while the parents are at work.

If you like teaching, this door is open any week of the year.

### Older people

Loneliness is the most invisible problem this group faces. An hour of conversation, reading to someone, listening to their stories, walking with them.

It needs no technical skills. It needs real, consistent presence.

### Community kitchens

Distributing food, helping with preparation, organising deliveries, registering families.

Two hours a week here has immediate feedback: you see how many meals were served. That motivates in a way other areas do not at the start.

### Migrant populations

Pereira has received significant migration. Projects supporting these families with regularisation, school integration, healthcare and work need help constantly.

If you speak other languages or have legal or psychological training, your contribution here is particularly scarce and valuable.

### Environment

Reforestation in rural areas, cleaning streams, environmental education in public schools.

For anyone who prefers working outdoors, it is the option with the best conditions and discrete, self-contained days.

## Call before you go

The most common mistake is assuming every foundation can always take volunteers. Each organization has its own moments, capacity and needs.

A call beforehand avoids wasted visits and saves both sides time.

## The advantage of a city this size

In large cities a volunteer can feel anonymous for months. In Pereira it is normal for coordinators and families to know you by name from your second visit.

That closeness builds bonds fast, and bonds are what keep people committed for years.

It also means local culture values continuous commitment over isolated action. An occasional visit is welcome; someone who turns up every fortnight for a year earns a different place entirely.
