You assume the interesting options are in the big cities or in international projects, and that where you live the options are limited.

It is an understandable perception and a wrong one. Practically every Colombian city has an ecosystem of active organizations needing permanent support. The information simply does not reach the people who could provide it.

## The eight categories you will find

**Early childhood.** Early stimulation, supplementary feeding, psychological support for families. This is where the foundations that last a lifetime get laid.

**Care homes.** Many residents have no active family network. A weekly visit changes the daily life of someone who gave a great deal and is now alone.

**Complementary education.** Academic support, culture, sport and psychosocial care outside school hours. Essential where families work and children would otherwise be alone.

**Community kitchens.** They serve between one and three hundred meals a day. For many families it is the difference between eating and not eating.

**Disability.** Therapies, inclusive education, workplace integration, support for family carers — services the public system does not reach.

**Migrant populations.** Regularisation, school integration, access to healthcare and work.

**Local environmental groups.** Watersheds, waste, environmental education in schools.

**Women in vulnerable situations.** From support around gender violence to economic autonomy.

## Every city has its own

Beyond those categories, each territory has its own issues that local organizations understand with contextual knowledge no national foundation can replicate.

Areas receiving high migration, coastal cities with fishing communities, territories with Indigenous populations, regions affected by the conflict. That specificity is an advantage: it means there is work only doable there.

## The principle that keeps you focused

**Depth before breadth.**

Choose **one** organization to start. Get to know it properly over six months. Only then consider diversifying.

Trying to support several from the outset scatters your attention and rarely builds a bond worth having. It is the most common mistake among people who start with a lot of enthusiasm.

## One useful exception

Do not restrict yourself strictly to your city while exploring. Many organizations have offices in several cities or accept remote volunteering.

That flexibility opens options you had not considered, especially if what you contribute is professional knowledge rather than physical presence.
