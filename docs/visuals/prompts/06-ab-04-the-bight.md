# AB-04 — The bight

> **Pack 06 · Why Cabuya** — index: [README.md](README.md).
> **Inherits** [STYLE_GUIDE.md](../STYLE_GUIDE.md): house style (§1), palette
> (§2), light/dark strategy and the edges rule (§3), the wordless rule (§4),
> dimensions (§5), asset paths (§6), accessibility (§7), the negative

> copy-paste ready and self-contained.

- **Target surface:** `/about` → the section on «coger la cabuya»
- **Type:** illustration
- **Priority:** medium
- **Purpose:** The page's most quietly persuasive point: Spanish already had the verb. *Coger la cabuya* means to catch on, to pick up the thread of a matter — the adoption verb existed before the protocol did. A bight of rope held open, offered, waiting to be taken, is that idiom without translating it. It also has to survive being read by somebody who does not speak Spanish, which is why it is a shape and not a pun.
- **Aspect & dimensions:** 3:2 · 720 × 480 displayed · generate @2x (1440 × 960)
- **Light/dark strategy:** transparent
- **Prompt:**
  > **HOUSE STYLE —** Fine hand-drawn line work in the manner of a rope-maker's technical drawing: laid rope with its three strands legible, line weight varying the way real cordage does. Flat 2D, no perspective, no lighting, no photographic reference. Print-quality line work with a faint natural paper tooth.
  >
  > **ONE FILE, BOTH THEMES —** The drawing is placed on a warm-white page (`#FAF9F6`) *and* on a dark green one (`#082A24`), from the same file, so it must carry its own tonal range instead of borrowing the page's. Draw it the way an engraving reads on either paper: deep forest-ink (`#0B3D32`) contours and shadow, with ivory (`#F6F3ED`) highlights worked into the forms themselves. A drawing in one flat value disappears on one of the two grounds.
  >
  > **SUBJECT —** A single length of laid rope lying across the frame, with a generous **open bight** in the middle — a loop held open, wide enough that a hand could pass through it, the two legs of the rope running away to the left and to the right and tapering to nothing inside the frame. The loop is *offered*, not tied: no knot, no hitch, nothing closed. It should read as a rope waiting to be picked up.
  >
  > The bight is drawn slightly off-centre, a little left of the middle, so the composition has somewhere to go rather than sitting symmetrically. The rope is calm — one confident curve, no coils, no tangle, no spiral.
  >
  > **One fique-gold (`#C79A4A`) fibre** runs along the rope's length and around the full inside of the bight, so the eye follows the loop without being told to. One continuous thread, drawn as a thread; the only chroma in the image.
  >
  > **EDGES / BACKGROUND —** Fully transparent ground: no page colour, no fill, no border, no frame, no vignette. **Nothing touches or crosses an edge** — both ends of the rope terminate inside the canvas and feather to nothing, with a clear margin of at least 8% of the canvas on all four sides containing no ink at all.
  >
  > **AVOID —** any border, frame, rectangle, box or outline; a filled or tinted ground; a closed knot, hitch, noose or tied loop; coils, tangles or spirals; **human figures, hands, arms or faces** — the offer is in the shape of the loop, and a hand would make it a stock photograph; arrows or motion lines; elements near or touching the edges; **any text, letters, numerals or labels whatsoever**; photographic realism; 3D render; gloss or metal; drop shadows; a second accent colour; clip-art; flat-vector illustration; watermark.
