### RG-01 — Registry: the woven net

- **Target surface:** `/registry` → header, beside the intro and the filters
- **Type:** illustration
- **Priority:** high
- **Purpose:** A registry of organisations, drawn as a net: knots joined by cord, each knot the same size as every other. No knot is bigger, more central, or more finished than the rest. This is the whole political claim of the registry made visual — it lists, it measures, and it does not rank.
- **Aspect & dimensions:** 3:2 · 720 × 480 displayed · generate @2x (1440 × 960)
- **Light/dark strategy:** matched pair
- **Prompt:**
  > **HOUSE STYLE —** Fine hand-drawn line work in the manner of a botanical plate or a rope-maker's technical drawing: twisted fibres, braided cords, knots, and threads that bind. Line weight varies the way real cordage does — a thick laid rope resolving into thin individual filaments. Monochrome forest ink (`#0B3D32`) on warm white paper (`#FAF9F6`), with **one single fique-gold strand** (`#C79A4A`) traced through the composition — one continuous thread, never a fill, never a second accent. Calm, warm, technical, editorial. Flat 2D. Generous margins. Print-quality line work with a faint natural paper tooth.
  >
  > **SUBJECT —** A section of hand-knotted net, drawn the way a fishing-net repair manual would show it: clear square or diamond mesh, each intersection a visible knot, the knots all the same size and drawn with equal care. The net is not perfectly regular — it is handmade, so the mesh varies slightly — and it is unfinished at two edges where the cords continue as loose ends into open paper. One cord running diagonally through the mesh is fique gold (`#C79A4A`), passing through several knots without being more prominent than them. No frame, no float, no fish, no water.
  >
  > **EDGES / BACKGROUND —** Background filled edge to edge with **exactly `#FAF9F6`**; all four corners sample to that hex; the mesh dissolves into the ground at the edges rather than being cut off. No border or outline.
  >
  > **AVOID —** any border, frame, rectangle, box or outline; elements touching or clipped by the edges; hard straight edges; gradients other than the soft edge fade; glossy or 3D render; photographic realism; neon or saturated colour; drop shadows; busy composition; watermark; any logo; **any text, letters, numerals or labels**; flat-vector "corporate memphis" people; clip-art; emoji; a second accent colour; human faces or identifiable people; disaster imagery; any knot drawn larger, brighter or more central than the others.
  >
  > **DARK VARIANT —** Same composition. Ivory ink (`#F6F3ED`) on **exactly `#082A24`**; the diagonal cord stays `#C79A4A`.

- **Suggested asset path:** `public/images/visuals/registry/registry-net.webp` + `-dark.webp`
- **Alt text (EN):** `alt=""` — decorative.
- **Alt text (ES):** `alt=""` — decorativa.
- **Integration note:** Registry header, `loading="eager"`, explicit width/height. Must not appear on individual publisher pages.
- **Status:** to-generate

### RG-02 — Governance: hands and cords

- **Target surface:** `/governance` → header
- **Type:** illustration
- **Priority:** medium
- **Purpose:** Governance pages are where a reader decides whether a project can be trusted with a decision. Hands working cord together — plural, equal, no one holding the whole thing — reads as shared stewardship without claiming an authority nobody granted us.
- **Aspect & dimensions:** 3:2 · 720 × 480 displayed · generate @2x (1440 × 960)
- **Light/dark strategy:** matched pair
- **Prompt:**
  > **HOUSE STYLE —** Fine hand-drawn line work in the manner of a botanical plate or a rope-maker's technical drawing: twisted fibres, braided cords, knots, and threads that bind. Line weight varies the way real cordage does — a thick laid rope resolving into thin individual filaments. Monochrome forest ink (`#0B3D32`) on warm white paper (`#FAF9F6`), with **one single fique-gold strand** (`#C79A4A`) traced through the composition — one continuous thread, never a fill, never a second accent. Calm, warm, technical, editorial. Flat 2D. Generous margins. Print-quality line work with a faint natural paper tooth.
  >
  > **SUBJECT —** Three or four pairs of hands, drawn only to the wrist, working the same length of cord — one pair holding tension, one splicing, one feeding new fibre in. The hands are drawn as anatomical line studies, unadorned, without jewellery, watches, sleeves or any other marker of a specific person. They are arranged around the cord with no obvious hierarchy: no pair is central, no pair is doing the "real" work. The gold strand runs the length of the cord they are all working on.
  >
  > **EDGES / BACKGROUND —** Background filled edge to edge with **exactly `#FAF9F6`**; corners sample to that hex; the wrists fade out rather than being cut by a hard edge. No border or outline.
  >
  > **AVOID —** any border, frame, rectangle, box or outline; elements touching or clipped by the edges; hard straight edges; gradients other than the soft edge fade; glossy or 3D render; photographic realism; neon or saturated colour; drop shadows; busy composition; watermark; any logo; **any text, letters, numerals or labels**; flat-vector "corporate memphis" people; clip-art; emoji; a second accent colour; **faces, heads, or any identifiable person**; jewellery, watches or clothing detail; disaster imagery; any composition where one pair of hands is clearly in charge.
  >
  > **DARK VARIANT —** Same composition. Ivory ink (`#F6F3ED`) on **exactly `#082A24`**; the strand stays `#C79A4A`.

- **Suggested asset path:** `public/images/visuals/governance/governance-hands.webp` + `-dark.webp`
- **Alt text (EN):** `alt=""` — decorative.
- **Alt text (ES):** `alt=""` — decorativa.
- **Integration note:** Page header, `loading="lazy"`, explicit width/height. **Review before shipping:** hands are the one place this pack approaches depicting people, and the style guide's no-faces rule is not negotiable.

### RG-03 — Join: the added strand

- **Target surface:** `/join` → header, above the contribution paths
- **Type:** illustration
- **Priority:** medium
- **Purpose:** The funnel page. A rope mid-splice, with one new strand being laid in — the network is not complete, and adding to it is a normal operation rather than an application to a club.
- **Aspect & dimensions:** 3:2 · 720 × 480 displayed · generate @2x (1440 × 960)
