# AB-03 — The splice with no centre

> **Pack 06 · Why Cabuya** — index: [README.md](README.md).
> **Inherits** [STYLE_GUIDE.md](../STYLE_GUIDE.md): house style (§1), palette
> (§2), light/dark strategy and the edges rule (§3), the wordless rule (§4),
> dimensions (§5), asset paths (§6), accessibility (§7), the negative
> boilerplate (§8), the entry schema (§9). The **Prompt** field below is
> copy-paste ready and self-contained.

- **Target surface:** `/about` → the section on why the name belongs to nobody
- **Type:** illustration
- **Priority:** high
- **Purpose:** The governance claim, and the one an international reader needs most: the protocol is not named after an application, a city or an event, so nothing about adopting it requires anyone's permission or credits anyone's brand. A splice where several ropes join with no hub, no hierarchy and no visible owner is that argument as a shape. It is the counter-image to every network diagram with a server in the middle.
- **Aspect & dimensions:** 1:1 · 560 × 560 displayed · generate @2x (1120 × 1120)
- **Light/dark strategy:** transparent
- **Prompt:**
  > **HOUSE STYLE —** Fine hand-drawn line work in the manner of a rope-maker's technical drawing: twisted fibres, braided cords, splices and knots, the over-and-under always legible. Line weight varies the way real cordage does. Flat 2D, no perspective, no lighting, no photographic reference. Print-quality line work with a faint natural paper tooth.
  >
  > **ONE FILE, BOTH THEMES —** The drawing is placed on a warm-white page (`#FAF9F6`) *and* on a dark green one (`#082A24`), from the same file, so it must carry its own tonal range instead of borrowing the page's. Draw it the way an engraving reads on either paper: deep forest-ink (`#0B3D32`) contours and shadow, with ivory (`#F6F3ED`) highlights worked into the forms themselves. A drawing in one flat value disappears on one of the two grounds.
  >
  > **SUBJECT —** **Five separate ropes** entering the square from five different directions — not evenly spaced, not radially symmetrical, each one arriving at its own angle as if from somewhere different. They meet in the middle third of the frame and are **spliced into one another**: each rope's strands opened and tucked under the others, the way a real splice is made, so that the join is a weave rather than a bundle.
  >
  > **The join must have no centre.** No hub, no ring, no knot at the middle, no point where all five converge, nothing sitting on top of the others. Each rope goes under some neighbours and over others, so no single one is dominant and the eye cannot find a first or a last. Draw it so that if you covered any one rope, the remaining four would still hold together — that is the entire argument of the picture.
  >
  > All five ropes are the same weight and the same drawing quality. **One of them carries a fique-gold (`#C79A4A`) fibre** running along its length and continuing through the splice into a *different* rope on the other side — one continuous golden thread crossing the join, showing that the material passes between them rather than belonging to any one. It is the only chroma in the image.
  >
  > **EDGES / BACKGROUND —** Fully transparent ground: no page colour, no fill, no circle, no ring, no border, no frame, no vignette. **Nothing touches or crosses an edge** — all five ropes terminate inside the canvas and taper to nothing, with a clear margin of at least 8% of the canvas on all four sides containing no ink at all. They enter from directions, not from edges.
  >
  > **AVOID —** any border, frame, rectangle, box, circle, ring or outline; a central hub, node, dot, star or point of convergence; radial symmetry or an evenly spaced fan; anything resembling a network diagram, a nodes-and-edges graph, a constellation or connection lines; arrows; a filled or tinted ground; elements near or touching the edges; **any text, letters, numerals or labels whatsoever**; logos or marks; human figures, hands or faces; photographic realism; 3D render; gloss or metal; drop shadows; a second accent colour; clip-art; flat-vector illustration; watermark.
